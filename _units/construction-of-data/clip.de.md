A short video animation on Lea's story and why we are all creators of data

Through the discovery of her grandmother's forgotten archives, Lea learns that preserving memories involves more than saving objects. The animation explores how cultural digital data are created, documented, interpreted, and shared, while emphasizing the importance of ethics, consent, and long-term access to cultural heritage.
